## English

I build vision software for autonomous UAVs: real-time object detection and tracking, vision-based target tracking and guidance, and visual-inertial localization (SLAM) in GNSS-denied environments, developed and tested with ROS, Gazebo and ArduPilot SITL.

### 🛩️ Now

- **CTO at Hamidiye Savunma Teknolojileri.** I'm responsible for image processing and guidance software for **VAKKAS**, an interceptor UAV that neutralizes target drones by launching a net (NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano + Cube Orange+, ArduPilot, pymavlink/MAVROS).
- **FocusGuard.** I lead development of an Android focus app that tracks gaze on the device (MediaPipe + a per-user SVM). It reaches 97.3% focus/away accuracy on ground-truth-labeled recordings and is being prepared for Google Play.
- **Teaching.** I'm an instructor at the T3 Foundation and a project advisor at Deneyap Türkiye, teaching AI and technology to school students.

- **TEKNOFEST 2026 Fighter UAV – Hunter Drone:** finalist; first place in the simulation flight stage (100/100)

- **İHA-1 commercial UAV pilot license**, plus FPV drone pilot training (Skydagger, 2026)
